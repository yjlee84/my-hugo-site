I am an economist and data analyst with experience in economic research, policy analysis, and applied quantitative work.

I completed the Analysis and Policy in Economics master's program at the Paris School of Economics and worked as a Consultant at the OECD. Previously, I worked as a Data Analyst at the Institute for International Economic Studies (IIES), Stockholm University, and as a Teaching Assistant at the University of Toronto.

My interests include economic history, development, trade, inequality, data analysis, and finance.
