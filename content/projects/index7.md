As a Consultant at the OECD, I modernised and integrated an R-based data-processing pipeline for the [Healthcare Quality and Outcomes](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/health-care-quality-and-outcomes.html) data collection. The workflow standardises multi-year country submissions, runs automated checks for structural errors, invalid entries, duplicates, missing observations, logical inconsistencies, zero denominators, and unusual year-on-year changes, and then calculates indicators and confidence intervals. It also generates validation reports and review dashboards and prepares validated results for database upload, reducing reliance on manual spreadsheet processing and improving the reproducibility and consistency of cross-country health data.
