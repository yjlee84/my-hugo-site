This project measures and compares colonial transfers from British India, the Dutch East Indies, and French Indochina, examining how fiscal capacity, colonial institutions, and historical conditions shaped the transfer of public resources to European metropoles. I constructed British Indian fiscal series from parliamentary financial statements using archival research, OCR, automated table extraction, and data validation, and harmonized them with Dutch and French reconstructions for comparison relative to public revenue and GDP. The results reveal distinct trajectories: transfers became more systematic as fiscal capacity expanded in British India, were closely tied to commodity production and colonial budget surpluses in the Dutch East Indies, and were more volatile in French Indochina, where wartime metropolitan spending sometimes produced net inflows.

*Co-supervised by* **Thomas Piketty** *and* **Kevin H. O'Rourke**.
