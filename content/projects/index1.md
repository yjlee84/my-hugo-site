This project measures and compares colonial transfers from British India, the Dutch East Indies, and French Indochina, examining how fiscal capacity, colonial institutions, and historical conditions shaped the transfer of public resources to European metropoles. I constructed British Indian fiscal series from parliamentary financial statements using archival research, OCR, automated table extraction, and data validation, and harmonized them with Dutch and French reconstructions to compare transfers relative to public revenue and GDP.

*Co-supervised by* **Thomas Piketty** *and* **Kevin H. O'Rourke**.
